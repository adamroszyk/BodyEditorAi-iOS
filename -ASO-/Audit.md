## 2. Critical Issues

### ISSUE 1: Keywords Filled with Irrelevant/Competitor Terms

Many locale keyword fields contain terms unrelated to body/photo editing:

- **en-US**: pmo, minimax, pick up lines, pickup  
- **ja**: nudity, blotato, polypal, armocromia test, combyne  
- **ru**: covenant eyes, stop motion studio, quit corn, blocksite  
- **zh-Hans**: try on clothes, air tag detector, sweet baby girl, dr fone, k12 parent portal  
- **id**: spicy chat ai, my love, talkie  
- **no**: ChatGPT, character ai, candy ai, drawing pad, rizz app  
- **it**: polly buzz, joi, video chat, final fantasy  
- **cs**: find my air tag, sun tan city, detector de rastreadores  
- **ca**: freestyle libre 2, glean  
- **hr**: cross hair, k plus, lift off, summernails, tap roulette  

**Impact**: Wasted keyword space; diluted relevance for body/beauty/photo search.

### ISSUE 2: 34 of 39 Locales Have Untranslated Descriptions

Only **5** locales have localized description copy: **vi**, **fr-FR**, **ms**, **th**, **tr**.  
The other **34** use the same English description. Descriptions are indexable and affect conversion in non-English stores.

### ISSUE 3: Name & Subtitle Not Localized

All 38 non–en-GB locales use the same English name and subtitle. Localizing for major markets (DE, FR, ES, PT-BR, JA, KO, ZH) would improve discovery and relevance.

### ISSUE 4: Promotional Text Empty or Corrupted

Many locales have no promo text or end with "loox max c ai". Promo text is indexable and editable without a new version.

### ISSUE 5: What's New Generic or Empty

- v1.9 live: "- Icon updates" everywhere.  
- v2.0 pending: empty for most locales; en-GB has "- Improved onboarding / Bug fixes / More styles available".

### ISSUE 6: Duplicate Keywords

- **ko**: 렌즈 x3  
- **tr**: photoshop x2  
- **ro**: cv europass x2  

### ISSUE 7: en-GB Keywords Under-Optimized

Primary locale uses 98/100 characters and misses high-value terms: body ai, body tune, body beauty, body sculptor, body filter, body morph, body reshaping.

## 5. Description Translation Guidance (34 un-localized locales)

### 5.1 Priority Tiers

| Tier | Locales | Rationale |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Tier 1** | ja, ko, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, de-DE, es-ES, pt-BR | High revenue/downloads; strong local search behavior. fr-FR already done. |
| **Tier 2** | ru, ar-SA, hi, it, nl-NL, pl, sv, no, da | Major European and MENA/India markets. |
| **Tier 3** | id, ro, hu, cs, sk, fi, el, hr, uk, he, ca, en-CA, en-AU, es-MX, pt-PT, th, vi, tr, ms | Remaining locales; some share language with Tier 1/2. |

### 5.2 Translation Approach

1. **Source**: Use the English v1.9 description as the single source of truth.  
2. **Preserve structure**: Keep sections (TL;DR, Why You’ll Love…, How It Works, Pro Perks, CTA, Privacy).  
3. **Keyword density**: In each locale, keep or add the proposed keywords from Section 4 in the first 2–3 paragraphs and in headings.  
4. **Native phrasing**: Prefer natural search phrases over literal translation where they differ (e.g. “body tune” vs “body editor” in local usage).  
5. **Legal**: Keep Privacy Policy & Terms URL and any required disclosures unchanged or adapt only the surrounding sentence.  
6. **QA**: Have a native speaker or professional translator spot-check Tier 1 and Tier 2 before release.

### 5.3 Already Localized (no change needed)

- vi, fr-FR, ms, th, tr  

### 5.4 Reuse / Variants

- **es-MX**: Can start from es-ES and adapt for Mexican Spanish.  
- **pt-PT**: Can start from pt-BR and adapt for European Portuguese.  
- **en-CA, en-AU**: Use en-US or en-GB as base and adjust spelling (e.g. colour, behaviour) and minor wording if desired.
