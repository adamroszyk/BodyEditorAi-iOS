# High-Level Improvement Ideas (3–5 per Goal)

This document lists concise, high‑leverage ideas to pursue next. Each goal has 3–5 specific implementation candidates.

---

## 1) Improve Impressions → Downloads (ASO / Store Conversion)
- **App Preview Video (15–30s)**: show 3–4 dramatic before/after transformations with clear text overlays for benefits.
- **First Screenshot Rework**: lead with most dramatic transformation + “FREE” badge; simplify copy to 3–5 words.
- **Keyword‑Rich Subtitle**: include high‑value terms not in title (e.g., “body editor, face retouch, AI photo”).
- **Seasonal Screenshot Set**: swap to seasonal “New Year / Summer body” variants quarterly.
- **Custom Product Pages**: segment by intent (fitness, beauty, creators) and run Apple Search Ads tests.

## 2) Improve Free → Paid Conversion
- **Value Demo Before Paywall**: blur/watermark full result; unlock HD via paywall.
- **Smart Paywall Timing**: show paywall immediately after first successful transformation.
- **Extend Free Trial**: increase free generations to 3–5 to reach “aha” moment.
- **Trust Signals**: add “Cancel anytime”, ratings badge, privacy messaging, secure payment icons.
- **Exit‑Intent Offer**: 50% off or 3‑day trial when user tries to dismiss paywall.

## 3) Improve Product Stickiness (Retention)
- **Saved Gallery / My Transformations**: persistent library with quick re‑edit and share.
- **“Continue Editing” Reminders**: save sessions and prompt next day via push.
- **Daily Challenges / Streaks**: themed prompts + streak rewards.
- **Favorites & Collections**: save prompts and group them into quick‑use sets.
- **In‑App “What’s New”**: changelog + new feature highlights to drive re‑opens.

## 4) Increase LTV (Popcorn Pricing for Power Users)
- **Tiered Subscriptions**: Basic / Pro / Unlimited with clear limits and benefits.
- **Generation Packs**: one‑time add‑ons for high‑usage users (e.g., 20, 50, 100 packs).
- **Usage‑Based Upgrade Prompts**: “You used 80% of your weekly limit” nudge to upgrade.
- **Annual Plan Anchor**: highlight savings vs weekly/monthly to lift LTV.
- **Creator / Power Tier**: premium tier with priority queue + higher quality exports.

## 5) Make the Product More Viral
- **Branded Watermark**: “Made with Body Editor AI” on free exports with App Store CTA.
- **Before/After Social Export**: one‑tap 9:16 / 1:1 templates optimized for Reels/TikTok.
- **Share‑to‑Unlock**: share a result to unlock 2 extra free generations.
- **Referral Program**: invite friends, both get bonus generations.
- **Viral Templates**: trend formats like “Glow Up”, “Expectation vs Reality”.

## 6) Decrease Spending (Inference & Infra Cost)
- **Reduce Parallel Generations**: 6 → 3, add “Generate More” button.
- **Image Compression**: downscale uploads to 1024–1536px max before sending.
- **Preview Quality Tier**: low‑res preview, HD only on export.
- **Smart Caching**: cache similar prompts/transformations per session.
- **Prompt Token Optimization**: shorten prompts, remove redundancies.

## 7) Make Product More Engaging (Interactive Editing)
- **AI Photo Analysis on Upload**: detect lighting, pose, clarity, and surface 3 recommended fixes.
- **Guided Enhancement Flow**: Upload → Analysis → Suggested Prompts → Pick → Save.
- **Intensity Slider**: control effect strength (0–100%) per enhancement.
- **Multi‑Enhancement Presets**: “Dating Profile”, “Beach Ready”, “Studio Portrait”.
- **Quick A/B Compare**: fast toggle between original and AI output with side‑by‑side view.
